1. The basics

OpenClaw connects an AI to the apps you already use

OpenClaw is a self-hosted gateway — a small program that connects an AI agent to chat apps like Telegram or WhatsApp.

  • 🚀 Runs on your own server, your rules
  • 🔗 One gateway can serve 20+ chat apps at once
  • 🤖 Remembers you between conversations
  • 📜 Free and open source (MIT license)

Chat apps OpenClaw can plug into

WhatsAppTelegramSlackDiscordSignaliMessageMatrixZaloWebChatGoogle ChatIRCLINEQQWeChat+ more
"The gateway is just the control plane — the product is the assistant."
Emphasize: OpenClaw itself is plumbing. What students actually experience is a friendly assistant that happens to live inside apps they already have installed.

Before we touch anything

💳 A quick, honest word about cost

Server (Amazon Lightsail)

The plan recommended for OpenClaw is $24/month, billed by the hour.

AWS's Lightsail free-trial offers only ever cover the small starter plans (a few dollars a month) — never the 4 GB plan OpenClaw needs. Offers change, so check AWS's current terms rather than assuming a fixed amount.

AI brain (Amazon Bedrock)

There is no free tier. Every message is billed by the token from the very first chat.

Usually a small fraction of a cent per message — but it's real money on a real card, not a sandbox.

That means today's build needs a credit card and an AWS account. So for the build steps, you'll watch on the big screen — no card required. Later, you'll send a real message to the bot we build, for free.
Be upfront and matter-of-fact. This is not a scare tactic — it's why the rest of the setup is instructor-led rather than hands-on for every student.

⚠️ A real lesson from the video

Never let your token show on screen

Even the person recording this official walkthrough almost leaked his own access token live on stream: "Oh no... we might be in trouble." He had to stop, hide his screen, and be more careful for the rest of the pairing step.

Your Gateway Token and messaging bot tokens are like a house key — anyone who has them can control your assistant. Never screen-share, screenshot, or paste them in a chat.

Watch this moment ↗ 14:46 (no screenshot shown here, on purpose)

Use this as a teachable moment: even experienced presenters make this mistake. That's exactly why we're not asking every student to handle a real token today.

⚠️ A real mistake from the video

Leave the default AI model alone

The presenter switched from the default model to a cheaper one to save money — and broke the chat with an error. He had to switch back: "This is not ideal, guys... I made a mistake there. I should have gone with the default model."

Lesson: the default model that ships with the blueprint is already tested to work. Only change it once everything is working, and change one thing at a time.

3. What the assistant remembers

Everything it knows about you lives in plain text files

No database, no hidden state — just markdown files the assistant reads and edits itself. You can open and read every one of them.

Compare to a person's notebook: SOUL/IDENTITY/USER = who they are, AGENTS/BOOT/TOOLS = their instructions, MEMORY = their diary.

4. How it gets new abilities

Tools vs Skills vs Plugins

LayerWhat it isWhen to useExample
🔧 ToolA function the agent can callIt needs to act: read, write, run code, searchread, exec, sessions_spawn
📜 SkillAn instruction sheet loaded into its promptA repeatable routine, no new codeweather, taskflow, diagram-maker
🧩 PluginExtends the program itself with new codeIt needs a whole new capability: a channel, a provider@openclaw/deepseek-provider
Think of it like a new employee: a Tool is a task they can do, a Skill is a printed instruction sheet you hand them, a Plugin is teaching them a whole new job.
Skills only need a markdown file — the lowest-effort way to extend OpenClaw.

5. How it stays proactive

TaskFlow · Cron · Heartbeat

🔧 TaskFlow

Runs multi-step jobs in the background. Remembers where it left off, even after a restart.

create → run → wait → resume → finish

⏰ Cron

Runs at an exact time, every time — like a phone alarm. "Scrape Twitter every 30 min", "Send a report at 8 AM."

💓 Heartbeat

Checks in on its own 2–4×/day against a checklist — like glancing at your phone a few times a day instead of staring at it.

Cron = exact timing. Heartbeat = batched checks, lower cost.
Heartbeat is cheaper because it bundles several small checks into one AI call instead of many separate ones.

7. Why bother self-hosting

Why OpenClaw?

🔒 Self-Hosted & Private

Your data stays on your own server. No third-party cloud access.

⚡ Pay for what you use

A Lightsail instance plus per-token AI cost — no forced subscription.

🔌 Multi-Channel

Telegram, WhatsApp, Slack — one agent, any surface.

🧩 Extensible

Skills, Plugins, TaskFlow — adapt it to almost any use case.

🤖 Proactive

Heartbeat + Cron mean it's always watching — you never have to ask.

📜 Open Source

MIT license. Full control. A growing community of plugins.

This is the "so what" slide — why go through all this setup instead of just using a chat app directly.
